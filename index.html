The core workflow

Less switching.
More room for the work.

Orbit connects the small steps of a research day: opening a paper, asking a question, checking a meeting and returning to the page.

Keep the paper in the conversation.

Read local or online PDFs beside ChatGPT. Resize the split, switch document tabs, and attach the current paper when your ChatGPT account and upload interface support it.

PDF reading + shared ChatGPT session

Start with a clear view of your day.

See today's schedule and unread-email summary on one home screen. Add local plans and meeting links, with reminders five minutes before events. Google summaries use an optional authorized connection.

Daily overview + local planning

Shape the workspace around you.

Reorder navigation, name your shortcuts and choose their icons. Fold the sidebar and PDF toolbar, pick a softer reading background, and adjust density and text size.

Custom navigation + focused reading

Let supported tasks run in the background.

An optional, token-authenticated localhost API and MCP bridge can read page text, retrieve authorized Google data and manage local plans while you keep working. The workspace source is open to extension.

Local API / MCP + editable integrations

Find what you just downloaded.

The download panel sits beside notifications. Follow progress, open a finished file, reveal its location or jump to the Downloads folder. Your download history stays on this device.

Progress + open file + reveal location

Move from the page to the meeting.

Keep useful webpages and conversations open between workspace sections. Store meeting links, open Zoom, or join in Orbit when the meeting and service support it.

Persistent pages + meeting shortcuts
Why use Orbit alongside Edge?

A research workflow,
already brought together.

Edge already offers PDF reading, split screen and Copilot. Orbit's appeal is how its workspace brings your chosen services, daily overview and reading tools together, with application source you can edit.

Your workflowIn OrbitIn Microsoft Edge
Read a paper with AIA dedicated PDF + ChatGPT layout, a resizable divider and shared ChatGPT sign-in across views.A built-in PDF reader, split-screen browsing and Copilot assistance; ChatGPT can also be used as a website.
Check your research dayA home overview combining optional Gmail / Calendar summaries, local plans and event reminders.A general-purpose browser: use calendar and email websites or integrations to assemble your daily workflow.
Arrange the reading spaceResearch shortcuts, saved navigation order, folding workspace / PDF controls and remembered reading backgrounds.Browser tabs and productivity tools, including split screen, for arranging web content.
Build your own integrationsThe workspace application source is editable. Its local API / MCP bridge exposes supported tasks without foreground clicking.Browser automation and extensions are available; Chromium is open source. Orbit publishes its own workspace and integration layer.

Choose Orbit for the assembled workflow. PDF rendering speed depends on the document, hardware and cache; no controlled performance comparison with Edge has been published.

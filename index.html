Why Orbit Workspace?

01Read with ChatGPT.

A dedicated paper-and-chat layout, shared sign-in and a resizable split keep the document in view.

02Your day, at a glance.

Optional Gmail and Calendar summaries, local plans and meeting reminders on one home screen.

03A workspace you can shape.

Custom navigation, focused reading controls and editable source, with an optional local API / MCP bridge.
